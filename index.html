Upcoming: Garden Festival · Tech Workshop · Cultural Fair

Community Bulletin Board

The city council has announced a rich series of community events scheduled throughout this year. Residents are warmly encouraged to participate and share their feedback with the organizing committee at each venue.

Local workshops on sustainable gardening, digital literacy, and arts & crafts are now open for registration. Seats are strictly limited, so early sign-up is strongly recommended for all interested participants.

The annual city parade will be held on the last Saturday of the month. Enthusiastic volunteers are needed to help coordinate activities and guide guests. Contact the events office for further information and sign-up details.

Emergency preparedness training sessions are available free of charge for all residents. These comprehensive sessions are conducted by certified professionals and remain open to every member of our community.
